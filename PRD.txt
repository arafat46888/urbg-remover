Use rembg with a lightweight compatible model initially.

Requirements:
- Remove image backgrounds.
- Preserve foreground details.
- Support transparent PNG output.
- Handle JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP.

- Limit upload size to 5 MB initially.
- Handle invalid images gracefully.
- Process images in memory where possible.
